# The Gnawdeep: zone plan

elvl 5-10. All cave: aggressive goblins under Warboss Grutt. Entered from the forest's hills and
from the mire's pools; rises toward Grutt's hall. See ../../world-plan.md and ../../quests-plan.md.

```
 Thornwood (the-hills)        Brackwater Mire (blackwater-pools)
          |                              |
     Forest Mouth                  Flooded Caves
          |                              |
    Sapper Tunnels ---------------- Fungus Deeps
                                         |
                                     Drum Halls -- Silver Seam (small)
                                         |
                                     Grutt's Hall -- Hall of the Goblin King (dungeon)
```

## Sizeable maps

All theme cave.

### forest-mouth
- elvl 5. The entrance from the forest.
- Places: the cave mouth; the wolf pens (L1, L3).
- Chains: L1, L3.
- Connects: sapper-tunnels; thornwood (the-hills).

### sapper-tunnels
- elvl 6. Tags `coral`. Narrow tunnels the sappers are digging.
- Places: sapper tunnels (L3); a sapper camp (S10).
- Chains: L3, S10.
- Connects: forest-mouth, fungus-deeps.

### flooded-caves
- elvl 6-7. Tags `wet`. The entrance from the mire.
- Places: flooded passages (L4); a glowworm grotto (S11).
- Chains: L4, S11.
- Connects: fungus-deeps; brackwater-mire (blackwater-pools).

### fungus-deeps
- elvl 7. Tags `cavities`. The goblins' mushroom farms.
- Places: mushroom farms (L3); the hexer's den and the shaman's fire (L3, named mobs).
- Chains: L3.
- Connects: sapper-tunnels, flooded-caves, drum-halls.

### drum-halls
- elvl 8. Tags `hub`. Where the tribes gather.
- Places: the gathering hall (L3); the wolf-master (L3, named mob); three hexed totems (S12).
- Chains: L3, S12.
- Connects: fungus-deeps, grutts-hall; silver-seam (small map).

### grutts-hall
- elvl 9-10. Tags `chamber`. The warboss's hall.
- Places: the guards' approach; Grutt's throne (boss, L3); Maren Holt's cell (L4); the sealed
  stair down to the Goblin King's hall.
- Chains: L3, L4.
- Connects: drum-halls; hall-of-the-goblin-king (dungeon).

## Small maps

- **silver-seam** (elvl 8): the expedition's silver seam (L4). Off drum-halls.

## Connections to other zones

- Thornwood: forest-mouth (the-hills).
- Brackwater Mire: flooded-caves (blackwater-pools).
- Hall of the Goblin King (dungeon): grutts-hall.

## Units

- Hostile: goblin, goblin-archer, -sapper, -hexer, -shaman, -wolfrider, -cutter, -mender, -sneak;
  worgs in the wolf pens; cave-bat, cave-spider; the hexer, shaman and wolf-master (named);
  Warboss Grutt (goblin-boss).
