# Brackwater Mire: zone plan

elvl 3-7. Lizardfolk swamp: the Reedtalkers near the forest, the Blackscales deeper in. Rises
from the forest edge south. See ../../world-plan.md and ../../quests-plan.md.

```
 Thornwood (southern-eaves)
          |
      Mire Edge
          |
  Reedtalker Village ---- Thornwood (old-growth)
          |
   Nesting Grounds ---- Blackwater Pools ---- Gnawdeep (flooded-caves)
          |   \
          |    Thornwood (pine-ridge)
          |
   Blackscale Marsh
          |
  Blackscale Village
```

## Sizeable maps

All theme glade with tags `wet`.

### mire-edge
- elvl 3. Where the forest gives way to the mire.
- Places: herb patches (S6); expedition survivors' camp (L4).
- Chains: S6, L4.
- Connects: reedtalker-village; thornwood (southern-eaves).

### reedtalker-village
- elvl 3-4. The Reedtalkers' village: the zone's hub.
- Places: the village; the elder's hut; fishing platforms.
- NPCs: Elder Sesh; quest givers; a rescued expedition member (L4).
- Chains: L2, L4, S6, S7, S8, S9.
- Connects: mire-edge, nesting-grounds; thornwood (old-growth).

### nesting-grounds
- elvl 4-5. The Reedtalkers' nesting grounds.
- Places: burned nests (L2); giant frog nests (S7).
- Chains: L2, S7.
- Connects: reedtalker-village, blackwater-pools, blackscale-marsh; thornwood (pine-ridge).

### blackwater-pools
- elvl 5-6. Deep, dark pools.
- Places: a deep pool (S8); the croc's wallow (S9); the flooded cave entrance (L4).
- Chains: S8, S9, L4.
- Connects: nesting-grounds; gnawdeep (flooded-caves).

### blackscale-marsh
- elvl 6. Blackscale territory.
- Places: Blackscale war-totems; raider camps; captive pens (L2).
- Chains: L2.
- Connects: nesting-grounds, blackscale-village.

### blackscale-village
- elvl 7. The Blackscales' village.
- Places: the village; the war-shaman's hut (named boss, L2).
- Chains: L2.
- Connects: blackscale-marsh.

## Connections to other zones

- Thornwood: mire-edge (southern-eaves), reedtalker-village (old-growth), nesting-grounds
  (pine-ridge).
- The Gnawdeep: blackwater-pools (flooded-caves).

## Units

- Hostile: giant-frog, swamp-crocodile (and the Old Croc), swamp-viper; Blackscale warriors,
  hunters and shamans; the Blackscale war-shaman (named boss).
- NCUs: Elder Sesh; Reedtalker villagers and fishers; expedition survivors.
