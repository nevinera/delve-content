# Thornwood: zone plan

elvl 0-4. The starting zone: forest full of wild creatures, with a few human camps. Rises from
the ranger outpost toward the hills (north) and the mire (south). See ../../world-plan.md and
../../quests-plan.md.

```
                 The Hills ---- Gnawdeep (forest-mouth)
                     |
 Woodcutters' Clearing -- Pine Ridge ---- Brackwater Mire (nesting-grounds)
          |
   Ranger Outpost
          |
      Old Growth ---- Brackwater Mire (reedtalker-village)
          |
   Southern Eaves ---- Brackwater Mire (mire-edge)
```

## Sizeable maps

### ranger-outpost
- elvl 0. Theme glade. The ranger outpost: the zone's hub.
- Places: the outpost stockade; a clearing; the expedition's notice board.
- NPCs: Ranger Hale; quest givers.
- Chains: L1, L4 (start).
- Connects: woodcutters-clearing, old-growth.

### woodcutters-clearing
- elvl 1. Theme glade. A woodcutters' camp in a clearing.
- Places: the woodcutters' camp (S1); a bear's den (S4); the boar run (L1).
- Chains: L1, S1, S4.
- Connects: ranger-outpost, pine-ridge, the-hills.

### old-growth
- elvl 2. Theme glade, tags `rough`. Deep woods.
- Places: old trees; a panther's den (S3).
- Chains: S3.
- Connects: ranger-outpost, southern-eaves; brackwater-mire (reedtalker-village).

### pine-ridge
- elvl 2-3. Theme autumn. A ridge.
- Places: an abandoned camp (S5); the expedition's empty camp (L4).
- Chains: S5, L4.
- Connects: woodcutters-clearing; brackwater-mire (nesting-grounds).

### southern-eaves
- elvl 2-3. Theme glade, tags `wet`. The forest's wet south edge.
- Places: the trappers' camp (L2, S2); snare lines (S2).
- Chains: L2, S2.
- Connects: old-growth; brackwater-mire (mire-edge).

### the-hills
- elvl 3-4. Theme autumn. Wooded hills to the north.
- Places: goblin snares; wolfrider patrols; a survivor's hide (L4); the cave mouth.
- Chains: L1, L4.
- Connects: woodcutters-clearing; gnawdeep (forest-mouth).

## Connections to other zones

- Brackwater Mire: southern-eaves (mire-edge), old-growth (reedtalker-village), pine-ridge
  (nesting-grounds).
- The Gnawdeep: the-hills (forest-mouth).

## Units

- Hostile: wolf, dire-wolf, panther (and the black panther), boar, black-bear; goblin wolfriders and
  sneaks in the-hills.
- Ambient: deer.
- NCUs: Ranger Hale; woodcutters; trappers; an expedition survivor.
