# Hall of the Goblin King: zone plan

Dungeon (private zone), elvl 10, solo. A cave complex below the Gnawdeep, reached by the sealed
stair from Grutt's hall. The end of L3. See ../../world-plan.md and ../../quests-plan.md.

```
 Gnawdeep (grutts-hall)
          |
    The King's Stair
          |
   The Royal Warrens ---- The Treasure Vault (small)
          |
    The Court of Bones
          |
   The Goblin King's Hall
```

## Maps

Theme cave throughout. Dungeon maps are mid-sized (around 60x60 to 100x100 cells): a run, not a
region.

### kings-stair
- elvl 10. Tags `coral`. The stair down: a winding descent with guard posts.
- Connects: royal-warrens; gnawdeep (grutts-hall).

### royal-warrens
- elvl 10. Tags `burrow`. The King's household: guards' barracks, kennels.
- Places: barracks; worg kennels.
- Connects: kings-stair, court-of-bones; treasure-vault (small map).

### court-of-bones
- elvl 10. Tags `hub`. The King's court: shamans and the royal guard.
- Places: the court; a bone throne-room antechamber.
- Connects: royal-warrens, goblin-kings-hall.

### goblin-kings-hall
- elvl 10. Tags `chamber`. The King's hall.
- Places: the throne; the King (dungeon boss).
- Connects: court-of-bones.

## Small maps

- **treasure-vault** (elvl 10): the King's hoard; optional loot. Off royal-warrens.

## Units

- Hostile: goblin-royal-guard; goblin shamans, hexers and menders; worgs; the Goblin King (boss).
