# demo-wild: quests plan

Basic stories: 4 long chains (8-12 quests) and 12 short ones (1-3 quests). Needs are
`zone[/map]: what`.

Zones: thornwood, brackwater-mire, gnawdeep. Dungeon: hall-of-the-goblin-king.

## Long chains

### L1. The Ranger's Watch
- Zones: thornwood, gnawdeep. elvl 0-5. 10 quests.
- Story: the forest's beasts are turning aggressive and coming close to the camps. Ranger Hale
  traces it to goblins driving them out of the hills and capturing wolves for their riders. Ends
  at the Gnawdeep's mouth.
- Quests:
  1. New at the Outpost (thornwood, 0): meet Ranger Hale.
  2. Too Close (thornwood, 1): wolves near the outpost.
  3. Tracks (thornwood, 1): their tracks come from the hills.
  4. The Boar Run (thornwood, 2): boars stampeding toward the woodcutters.
  5. Something Behind Them (thornwood, 2): goblin snares in the hills.
  6. Cut the Snares (thornwood, 3).
  7. Wolfriders (thornwood, 3): goblins riding captured wolves.
  8. The Cub (thornwood, 4): free a captured wolf pack's cubs.
  9. The Mouth (thornwood, 4): follow the riders to the cave mouth.
  10. First In (gnawdeep, 5): the wolf pens just inside.
- Needs:
  - thornwood: the ranger outpost; Ranger Hale (NPC); the hills with goblin snares; wolfrider
    patrols; the cave mouth.
  - gnawdeep: wolf pens near the forest entrance.
- Leads to: L3.

### L2. Scales and Smoke
- Zones: brackwater-mire, thornwood. elvl 3-7. 10 quests.
- Story: the Blackscales are raiding the Reedtalkers and burning their nests. The Reedtalkers'
  elder, Sesh, won't trust a human until you prove useful. Ends killing the Blackscale
  war-shaman.
- Quests:
  1. Smoke over the Mire (thornwood, 3): trappers see smoke to the south.
  2. Wary Welcome (brackwater-mire, 3): meet Elder Sesh.
  3. Prove It (brackwater-mire, 4): swamp beasts around the Reedtalker village.
  4. Burned Nests (brackwater-mire, 4): the raided nesting grounds.
  5. Blackscale Raiders (brackwater-mire, 5): drive them off.
  6. Their Totems (brackwater-mire, 5): the war-totems that rally them.
  7. Captives (brackwater-mire, 6): free Reedtalker captives.
  8. The War-Shaman (brackwater-mire, 6): who leads them.
  9. Into Blackscale Land (brackwater-mire, 7): the Blackscale village.
  10. Smoke Ends (brackwater-mire, 7): kill the war-shaman (named boss).
- Needs:
  - brackwater-mire: the Reedtalker village; Elder Sesh (NPC); nesting grounds; Blackscale war-totems;
    captive pens; the Blackscale village; the war-shaman (named boss).
  - thornwood: a trappers' camp at the forest's south edge.
- Leads to: L4 (the mire side).

### L3. The Gnawdeep
- Zones: gnawdeep, hall-of-the-goblin-king. elvl 5-10. 14 quests.
- Story: Warboss Grutt is uniting the cave tribes to raid both the forest and the mire. Push in
  from the forest side, break his lieutenants, and kill him in his hall. The turn: Grutt was
  only the King's warboss. Ends in the Hall of the Goblin King.
- Quests:
  1. Deeper (gnawdeep, 5): past the wolf pens.
  2. Sapper Tunnels (gnawdeep, 6): sappers digging toward the surface.
  3. Collapse Them (gnawdeep, 6).
  4. The Hexer (gnawdeep, 7): a goblin hexer cursing the tunnels (named mob).
  5. Mushroom Farms (gnawdeep, 7): burn their food.
  6. The Shaman's Fire (gnawdeep, 7): a goblin shaman (named mob).
  7. War Drums (gnawdeep, 8): the tribes are gathering.
  8. Break the Gathering (gnawdeep, 8).
  9. The Wolf-Master (gnawdeep, 8): the wolfriders' leader (named mob).
  10. Grutt's Hall (gnawdeep, 9): the way in.
  11. Guards (gnawdeep, 9).
  12. Warboss Grutt (gnawdeep, 10): the boss; his orders come from below.
  13. The Sealed Stair (gnawdeep, 10): open the way down.
  14. The Goblin King (hall-of-the-goblin-king, 10): the dungeon, and its king.
- Needs:
  - gnawdeep: sapper tunnels; mushroom farms; a gathering hall; the hexer, shaman and wolf-master
    (named mobs); Grutt's hall; Grutt (boss); the sealed stair down.
  - hall-of-the-goblin-king: the Goblin King (dungeon boss).

### L4. The Lost Expedition
- Zones: thornwood, brackwater-mire, gnawdeep. elvl 2-9. 9 quests.
- Story: a prospecting expedition went looking for silver in the hills and never came back. Its
  survivors are scattered across all three zones. Ends finding its leader, Maren Holt, alive
  deep in the caves.
- Quests:
  1. Overdue (thornwood, 2): the expedition's empty camp.
  2. The First Survivor (thornwood, 3): hiding in the hills.
  3. Split Up (thornwood, 3): half went south, half went in.
  4. South (brackwater-mire, 4): survivors in the mire.
  5. Reedtalker Rescue (brackwater-mire, 5): the Reedtalkers took one in.
  6. The Flooded Way (brackwater-mire, 6): the cave's mire entrance.
  7. In (gnawdeep, 7): through the flooded caves.
  8. The Silver Seam (gnawdeep, 8): what they found.
  9. Maren Holt (gnawdeep, 9): free her from the goblins.
- Needs:
  - thornwood: the expedition's camp; a survivor (NPC) in the hills.
  - brackwater-mire: survivors (NPCs); one in the Reedtalker village; the flooded cave entrance.
  - gnawdeep: the flooded caves; a silver seam; Maren Holt (NPC), held captive.

## Short chains

### S1. Wolves at the Woodpile
- thornwood, 1. 1 quest: clear wolves from the woodcutters' camp.
- Needs: thornwood: the woodcutters' camp.

### S2. Lost Snares
- thornwood, 1-2. 2 quests: recover a trapper's snares; deal with what was caught in them.
- Needs: thornwood: the trappers' camp; snare lines.

### S3. The Black Panther
- thornwood, 2-3. 2 quests: a panther stalking the camps; hunt it down (named mob).
- Needs: thornwood: a panther's den.

### S4. The Woodcutter's Axe
- thornwood, 1. 1 quest: a woodcutter's lost axe, in a bear's den.
- Needs: thornwood: a bear's den.

### S5. Smoke on the Ridge
- thornwood, 2-3. 3 quests: an abandoned camp on a ridge; who left it; what's camping there now.
- Needs: thornwood: a ridge with an abandoned camp.

### S6. Fever Herbs
- brackwater-mire, 3-4. 2 quests: herbs for a fevered trapper; a cure from the Reedtalkers.
- Needs: brackwater-mire: herb patches.

### S7. Reedtalker Eggs
- brackwater-mire, 4-5. 2 quests: eggs stolen by frogs; return them.
- Needs: brackwater-mire: giant frog nests.

### S8. The Sunken Idol
- brackwater-mire, 5. 1 quest: recover a Reedtalker idol from a pool.
- Needs: brackwater-mire: a deep pool.

### S9. The Old Croc
- brackwater-mire, 6. 2 quests: a huge crocodile eating Reedtalker fishers; kill it (named mob).
- Needs: brackwater-mire: the croc's wallow.

### S10. Sapper's Charges
- gnawdeep, 6. 2 quests: steal a sapper's charges; use them to seal a tunnel.
- Needs: gnawdeep: a sapper camp.

### S11. Glowworm Grotto
- gnawdeep, 7. 1 quest: a lost survivor's lantern in a glowworm grotto.
- Needs: gnawdeep: a glowworm grotto.

### S12. Hexer's Totems
- gnawdeep, 8. 3 quests: hexed totems sicken the tunnels; break three; the hexer's apprentice.
- Needs: gnawdeep: three hexed totems.
