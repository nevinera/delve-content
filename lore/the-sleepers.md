A smaller group, maybe a cult, reveres the Sleepers and the Dream.

In some places in the world, people suddenly appear: the Wakers. All they remember are bits and pieces of what feels like a dream.

Angeans believe Wakers are always [[humans]]. Really, which people wake in a place depends on the people who hold it, so in human lands they're human. Every player character is a Waker, and for now every one is human. Non-human player characters, if they come, may have a different origin.

The faith hints at the [[simulation]], but neither it nor anything else confirms it.
