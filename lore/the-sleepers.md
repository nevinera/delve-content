A smaller group, maybe a cult, reveres the Sleepers and the Dream.

In some places in the world, people suddenly appear. They're always [[humans]]. All they remember are bits and pieces of what feels like a dream. Every player character is one of them, so for now every player character is human. Non-human player characters, if they come, will have a different origin.

The faith hints at the [[simulation]], but neither it nor anything else confirms it.
