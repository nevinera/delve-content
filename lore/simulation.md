**A secret the game hints at but never confirms.**

The world is a simulation in the distant future, and the people who suddenly appear, every player character among them, were uploaded into it from the [[age-of-science]]. See [[the-sleepers]].

Hints stay vague and mythic in tone: dreams, waking, sleepers. This isn't science fiction, and nothing in the world says "simulation" or "upload".
