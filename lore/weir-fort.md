Weir Fort is a stone fort built across a weir on the Heartland river, in the Riverlands, between Wyeford and the estuary. The Tennelys' engineers built it in the colony's first years to hold the river and tax its barges. The river shifted its channel a few years later, and the fort was left half on dry ground and half in the water, with a barge sunk in its old lock.

## Who holds it

A smuggling ring holds it now. Its head is Edwin Haskard. He was the Tennelys' river engineer, then the Council's engineer on the great dike, until he was dismissed for selling its stone. He knows every sluice on the river. By opening and shutting them at night he floods his rivals' landings, strands the Council's toll barges, and raises the water under his own boats.

The ring brings goods upriver from ships off the coast, past the Council's tolls: wine, powder, Nordish furs and Angean silver. It sells grain the other way. Its partner at sea was a pirate captain, Ysolde Marrick, until the Council's navy took her. She's held on the prison hulks in Haven harbour. See [[the-hulks]].

The amphen, the frogfolk of the river marshes, suffer most from Haskard's floods. He keeps a few of them in the fort's cistern to work the sluices underwater.

## Who's there

From the way in to the heart, as tentative bosses for a dungeon of levels 20 to 27, for a tank and a healer:

1. **Selby One-Ear**, the ring's bargemaster, in the sunken barge in the old lock. He fights from the barge's tilted deck, with crew who come over the side.
2. **Old Mudjaw**, a huge snapping turtle Haskard feeds in the flooded cistern as a guard. The amphen workers are chained beside it. Freeing them after the fight opens the way on.
3. **Kit and Corra Heron**, sisters with harpoons, the ring's muscle. They fight on the weir's walkway, as a pair.
4. **Edwin Haskard**, the last boss, in the sluice house on top of the weir. He works the sluices as he fights, so the water rises and falls across the floor through the fight.

## How it flows

**Into it.** The Southern heartland's smuggler line ends with the reed camp's boatmaster and his map to an old weir fort downriver. In the Riverlands, two lines meet at the fort:

- *The great dike* (16–20, Dikemoot): the river's level changes without reason at night, and the sluices are found opened with keys only the engineers had.
- *Weir Fort* (20–25, Wyeford and Reedholt): Wyeford's barge-masters lose cargo, and Reedholt's amphen lose people.

The dungeon's own quests come from those two hubs:

- Wyeford's harbourmaster wants the stolen tolls back.
- Reedholt's elder asks you to free the amphen in the cistern.
- Dikemoot's honest engineer wants Haskard's sluice keys.

**Out of it.** Haskard's papers show two things:

- A buyer in Haven takes his contraband, through a harbour clerk.
- He has paid to break Ysolde Marrick out of the hulks.

That sends you to Haven and The Hulks, about level 24.
