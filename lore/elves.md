Elves stand for stability, nature and song.

Their conflicts with other peoples are with the [[humans]] and the smaller, more sympathetic peoples.

## History

About a thousand years ago the elves' world-spanning civilization of the [[age-of-light]] destroyed itself in [[lights-bane]]. Elves live to about 600, so it's within a few generations: for some, it's what their mother told them.

## Toward the colonies

Stand-offish, mostly over the logging and the damage to nature an expanding colony does.

## Where

On [[ithelmar]], they hold a high forest between the two human colonies, and an island off its east side.

## Ithelmar now

They see [[ithelmar]] as scarred but regrowing, and feel a deep responsibility to heal the damage their forebears did in [[lights-bane]]. Most young elves practically worship Nature these days, to their elders' consternation.
