- **The greenskin border:** the [[orcs]] and [[goblins]] raid one border of Ange's colony without end. See [[greenskin-border]].
- **Elves:** stand-offish toward the colonies, mostly over the logging and the damage to nature an expanding colony does. See [[elves]].
- **Halflings:** skittish, but sometimes met in human country on their wandertrail. See [[halflings]].
- **Dwarves:** trade openly with Ange but rarely come out, and humans don't trust them for it. See [[dwarves]].
- **Ange and Norda:** each has claimed its own part of the continent. They compete for resources rather than fight. See [[empire-of-ange]] and [[nordish]].
- **Ange's colony from within:** its rulers took power from [[lord-tennely]] by force and haven't kept their promises, and another revolution may be coming. See [[tennely-overthrow]].
