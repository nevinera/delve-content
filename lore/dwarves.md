Dwarves are long-lived, much more so than [[humans]]. They live in a few insular underground claves and one city, [[deephome]]. [[gnomes]] often live among them.

## Open questions

- What they stand for, and how they get along with the others.

## Toward the colonies

They trade openly with the [[empire-of-ange]], but rarely come out, and humans don't trust them for it.

## The Kingdom Beneath

They belong to the [[kingdom-beneath]], ruled by the [[king-below]]. [[lights-bane]] cut their tunnels under the sea, and they don't think of [[ithelmar]] as a place exactly: they call it what the elves did when they came up.
