One of the two human civilizations colonizing the continent, from [[angea]]. Modelled loosely on northern seafaring raiders and settlers.

Their relations with the [[empire-of-ange]] are cordial, if not cooperative.

Their realm is Norda. Ange and Norda have each claimed their own part of the continent. There are tensions, but no open conflict: they compete for resources rather than fight.
