Beside the [[humans]] and [[elves]] are smaller, more sympathetic peoples, caught up in the conflicts between them: [[gnomes]], [[halflings]], beastfolk, [[drakel]], [[centaurs]], [[taurel]], and probably more. Most of them are tribal. The [[dwarves]] are a people of their own.

## Open questions

- Which beastfolk there are. The game has bodies for boric, ursol and amphen.
