The demonic forces stand for corruption, horror and destruction. Their corruption is the dark side of the world's contrast with its beautiful magic.

## The greenskins

[[goblins]] summon demons for power, and the [[orcs]] came about that way. Goblins still deal with them and often use them as pawns. Orcs fight them.

## Where

Their hold on [[ithelmar]] is the old home of a goblin tribe that went too far: it summoned a demon prince it couldn't control. The prince has held that ground for about ten years. A few goblins still live scattered across it.

The prince's ground is a scar, not a zone of its own. It runs from beside the goblins' marsh south-west across three zones of greenskin country, to the island's southern tip. The prince's seat, the tribe's old home, is at the scar's north-east end, in the lake valley beside the marsh.
