The Tallstones are the ruins of a huge stone circle on the hill above [[haven]], the only stonework in the town older than the colony. Most of the stones have fallen. The ring is wide enough to hold a market, and the colonists built round it rather than over it.

The circle seems to affect the Ley, the flow of [[magic]] through the region. Spells cast inside the ring come out stronger or stranger. Compasses drift near the stones on some nights, and the Ley runs toward the circle the way water runs downhill.

The town's mages' guild, the Tallstones Lodge, built its hall against the ring and studies it. They don't agree on what it is or who raised it.

## Open questions

- Who raised it, and when: in the [[age-of-myth]], the [[age-of-light]], or before.
- Why it bends the Ley, and whether it can be made to again.
