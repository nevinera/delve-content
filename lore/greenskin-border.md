The greenskin tribes, [[orcs]] and [[goblins]], constantly send war parties and small armies against one border of the [[empire-of-ange]]'s colony. They've never broken through in numbers, but they've slowed the colony's expansion that way to a crawl.

The greenskins hold several zones south and south-east of Ange's colony on [[ithelmar]]. They hold the south-east marsh, the lake valley, the badlands and the southern tip. [[orcs]] and [[goblins]] live mixed in all four, in different balances: the marsh is mostly goblin. The [[demons]]' scar crosses the lake valley, the badlands and the southern tip. Orcs and demons raid each other along it, but they fight no battles.

The orcs' chief warcamp is on the badlands' west coast, and their raids go north from it into the gap. The goblins' seat is in the marsh, and from there they scheme with the orcs and with the demon prince next door.

The border they raid is the gap, a war front between the colony's harbour vale and the lake valley.
