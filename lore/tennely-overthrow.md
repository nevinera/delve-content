Rule of Ange's colony was taken by force from its first noble family, the Tennelys, under [[lord-tennely]]. The colony is now after that revolution, but its new leaders haven't lived up to their promises, and it's starting to feel as if another one is coming.

The old guard left behind thousands of former soldiers. Whether they're still loyal to the Tennely family isn't known.

## Open questions

- Who the new leaders are, and what they promised.
- What became of Lord Tennely and his family.
