After the [[age-of-myth]], the [[elves]] built a civilization that spanned the world, devoted to the [[lord-of-light]]. About a thousand years ago it destroyed itself, and much of the world with it, in [[the-cataclysm]].

Elves live to about 600, so to them this isn't distant history. For some it's what their mother told them.
