Haven is the capital of Ange's colony on [[ithelmar]], a port in the Harbour vale on the west coast. The Council sits there. See [[tennely-overthrow]].

It's wooden and new, not a stone city. It's bustling, it has an underbelly, and it smells of fish. Its dock takes two ships.

It has trainers for every profession, a bank, an auction house, a great many shops and two inns.

A mages' guild studies the ruins of a huge stone circle there, the [[tallstones]], which seems to affect the Ley, the flow of [[magic]] through the region.

The mood is jovial and frantic at once, with an undercurrent of worry about the Council.
