Halflings have a few villages in hidden, safe places. They're mostly farmers.

Halflings live about as long as [[humans]].

Halflings are skittish, but they're sometimes found in human country on their wandertrail, a coming-of-age journey a young halfling makes alone, between about 16 and 20. Families don't make it.

A few halfling families live alone on remote homesteads rather than in the villages. Those are the ones the war fronts reach first.
