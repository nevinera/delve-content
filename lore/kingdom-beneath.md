The whole kingdom of the [[dwarves]], ruled by the [[king-below]]. Until [[lights-bane]], dozens of dwarven cities were joined by massive, long tunnels under the sea, with trams running in them. Light's Bane destroyed the tunnels.

The dwarves of [[ithelmar]] haven't re-established contact since. They think it's just a matter of time and hard work. Whether the rest of the kingdom still exists isn't known.
