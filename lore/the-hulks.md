The hulks are three old warships of the Tennelys' navy, stripped of their masts and moored in Haven harbour as prisons. The Council keeps thieves, debtors, pirates and political prisoners there. Some of the last are old guard, Greycoats among them.

## The riot

Ysolde Marrick, a pirate captain, was taken by the Council's navy with a hold full of smuggled goods and put on the hulks. Her partner upriver, Edwin Haskard of [[weir-fort]], paid to break her out. His money bought the guards' rota from a harbour clerk, and the keys from the hulks' turnkey.

The breakout went wrong. The prisoners rose with Marrick's crew, took the guards hostage, and hold all three ships. Marrick means to fire the powder store under cover of the chaos and get away on a sloop.

The old-guard prisoners hold one deck of the middle hulk and stay out of it. They won't help the riot, and they won't help the Council's marines either.

## Who's there

From the shore end out, as tentative bosses for a dungeon of levels 24 to 30, for a tank and a healer:

1. **Ebbe Mabry**, the turnkey who sold the keys. He's locked himself in with the worst of the debtors' deck and runs it as his own little kingdom.
2. **Mother Barnacle**, in the flooded bilge of the oldest hulk. She's a huge crab that has grown fat on what's been thrown down there for years, and she has a swarm of young.
3. **The old guard's deck** isn't a fight. The Greycoat prisoners let you through if you leave them be. Jory Venn, moved here from Brightford's lock-up, shows you a way across to the last ship.
4. **Ysolde Marrick**, the last boss, on the last hulk's deck. Her crew board from the sloop alongside during the fight, and the powder store below is lit.

## How it flows

**Into it.** Weir Fort's papers send you to Haven about level 24. Harbour vale's line *The hulks* (24–30) gives the dungeon's quests:

- The harbourmaster wants the riot ended.
- The Council's marine captain wants the hostages freed.
- A pirate's sister wants word of her brother.

Haven's other lines point here too:

- Harbour gossip about Marrick's capture.
- Press gangs short of men because the marines are all at the hulks.

**The clerk.** The harbour clerk who sold the guards' rota is Varn Ostrey, the tithe collector the Northern heartland's reeve sent "to a desk in Haven". Players who did that line know him on sight. Finding him out is a quest in Haven after the dungeon, not a fight.

**Out of it.** Marrick's charts show her sea routes to the pirate isles off the south. That points toward the ship south and the landing (20–28), which players may have reached already. Jory Venn's help on the old guard's deck is the second time the Greycoats owe you, a thread for the endgame's second revolution.
