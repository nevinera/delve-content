The Greycoats are soldiers of the old guard who kept their grey Tennely coats after [[tennely-overthrow]] and went into the hills rather than serve the Council. Nobody knows how many there are. In the Northern heartland they keep to a burned hunting lodge in the hills above Tennely Hall, and leave grain on the steps of farms behind on their tithe.

The Council calls them bandits. They aren't: they don't rob farms or wagons, and they don't fight the militia. A captain leads them from the lodge, and Captain Moor the few who watch Tennely House. Some are in the Council's prisons, such as Jory Venn, held in Brightford's lock-up.

## Slade's band

Robbers in grey coats work the north road and the grain wagons to Tennely Hall, so the Greycoats take the blame. They're Slade's band, from a camp in the hills east of Tennely Hall. Slade keeps a tally of what he takes.
