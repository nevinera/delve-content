The present age, in the thousand years since [[the-cataclysm]]. [[humans]] call it the Age of Man. None of the other peoples would.

Humans have had a resurgence on [[angea]], thanks to their adaptability and their short lives beside elves' and dwarves', and they're in a great age of expansion.
