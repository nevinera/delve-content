Tennely House was the seat of the Tennely family, in the Southern heartland south of the Lake river. It burned in [[tennely-overthrow]]. Its cellars, the undercroft, survived.

## The family's secret

When [[lord-tennely]] built the house, nearly forty years ago, his diggers broke into a vault from the [[age-of-myth]] under the hill: the tomb of a warlord, Corvane, who had ruled a city there and bound his own spirit to his crown so as to outlast death. The vault was full of his treasure and guarded by what he'd bound to serve him.

The Tennelys took the treasure, sealed the vault behind a false wall, and told no one. That treasure paid for much of the family's rise. Only the family and a few of its oldest servants knew.

## Now

The Council took the house's ruins with the rest of the Tennely estates. A Council clerk in Sowerby hired a salvage crew under Mattock Brask to strip the undercroft of anything worth selling. Brask's crew found the false wall and broke through it.

What they let out has started working on them. The crew digs day and night now, and doesn't come up. Lights move in the ruins after dark, and farms nearby hear stone grinding under their fields.

The Greycoats want the vault sealed again. So does the Council, once it understands. Neither wants the other to learn what the family hid, or what paid for it.

## Who's there

From the top down, as tentative bosses for a dungeon of levels 17 to 24, for a tank and a healer:

1. **Mattock Brask**, the salvage boss. He's greedy, half in the warlord's thrall, and convinced the treasure is his by right of digging. His crew, in the same state, fight beside him.
2. **The Brass Seneschal**, a construct of brass and stone that kept the warlord's hall. It woke when the wall broke, and it serves whoever wears the crown.
3. **Gallowmaw**, the warlord's hound. It's a great beast bound to the tomb's door, starved for centuries, and it hunts by sound.
4. **Corvane, the Crowned in Stone**, the warlord, the last boss. His spirit lives in his crown. Every generation of greed in the house above fed him a little. He wants a body, and Brask's will do until a better one comes down the stairs.

## Ahead of time

Places and people that point to the undercroft before players can go in:

- **The Northern heartland:**
  - Old Bertil in Brightford says the old lord never let anyone into the cellars.
  - Grandsire Tobin Hallam says the old lord's luck "came out of the ground".
  - The Greycoat captain's letter names Tennely House.
- **The Southern heartland:**
  - Sowerby's salvage contract is posted for anyone to read, and it pays very well.
  - Brask's carters stop coming up to collect their pay.
  - Farms near the house hear grinding under their fields at night.
  - Captain Moor and a few Greycoats watch the ruins from the woods.
  - The Tennelys' old steward, Lowen Ashdown, now a beggar in Sowerby, gets drunk and says the house sits on "a dead man's money".
