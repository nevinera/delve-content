The world is a contrast between pleasant lands at peace and devastating war fronts, and between beautiful magic and horrifying demonic corruption.

The great powers are [[humans]], [[elves]], the [[demons]], the [[orcs]] and [[goblins]], and the [[primals]].

The conflicts between peoples, racial and diplomatic, are between the humans and the elves, and smaller groups of more sympathetic peoples. The orcs and goblins aren't one of those peoples: everyone else sees them as monsters.

## Open questions

- Which smaller peoples are there?
- What the war fronts are, and who fights on them.
