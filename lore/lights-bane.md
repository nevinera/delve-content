Light's Bane was some kind of technomagical cataclysm that ended the [[age-of-light]]. The [[elves]] destroyed their own civilization with it, and much of the world.

## Open questions

- What it was, and what caused it.
- What it left behind.

It also destroyed the [[dwarves]]' tunnels under the sea. See [[kingdom-beneath]].
