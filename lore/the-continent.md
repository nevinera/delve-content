The continent we're building is really a very large island. It has several large mountains, joined by a chain that curves across its middle. Its weather is much harder at the north end.

- **West coast:** Ange's colony. See [[empire-of-ange]].
- **North and north-east:** the [[nordish]] lands of Norda.
- **South and south-east of Ange:** several zones held by the greenskins. See [[greenskin-border]].
- **Between the two colonies:** a high forest of the [[elves]].
- **Off the east side:** an island of the elves.
- **Under the mountains:** the [[dwarves]]' claves, and [[deephome]] under the second tallest.

## Open questions

- Its name, which differs by language.
