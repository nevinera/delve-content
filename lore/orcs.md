Orcs, with the [[goblins]], stand for greed, aggression and barbarism.

They aren't a sympathetic people. They're horrifying monsters that eat children. They have a culture of their own, but the other peoples, fairly, see them as intelligent monsters rather than another culture.

Most orcs are enemies. A few small groups are NPCs rather than mobs.
