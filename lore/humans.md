Humans stand for civilization, colonization and change.

Their conflicts with other peoples are with the [[elves]] and the smaller, more sympathetic peoples.

## Now

In the [[age-of-man]], as they call it, humans have risen again on [[angea]], thanks to their adaptability and short lives beside elves' and dwarves', and are expanding. Two of their civilizations, the [[empire-of-ange]] and the [[nordish]], are colonizing the continent we're building. They've been here about 40 years, and their colony heartlands are stable and safe.
