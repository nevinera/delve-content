A separate continent to the south of [[ithelmar]], reached only by ship. It's warmer than Ithelmar. The [[primals]] hold a volcano on the far side of its mountains, and highlands full of waterfalls beside it. [[the-dragon]] lives in a mountain there, a high-level place.

A whole corner of it is [[dead-colony]]'s undead wasteland. Off one end lie the pirate isles, an archipelago.
