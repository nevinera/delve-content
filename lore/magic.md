Magic is a mystery. It comes from the world and the stars, and it comes to some more than others.

It's an art, not a science. In practice it uses the same faculties as architecture or programming: seeing in space, chaining logic, and sheer mental scope.

Magic flows through the land. The flow through a region is called the Ley. Some places bend it, such as the [[tallstones]] above [[haven]].

It resists every attempt to categorize it. The records suggest its effects shift over time.

By most accounts there was none in the [[dawn-times]].
